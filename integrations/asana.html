Project Management Integration

Reliabs + Asana

Product bets require cross-functional execution — design, marketing, legal, and ops all carry dependencies. Reliabs connects Asana project timelines to your roadmap bets so delivery risk is visible before it becomes a launch blocker.

What This Integration Does

The Reliabs + Asana integration reads your Asana projects, tasks, and milestones and connects them to corresponding product bets in Reliabs. When cross-functional work slips, Reliabs surfaces the dependency risk in your roadmap context — automatically.

Project-to-Bet Linking

Setup

How to Connect Asana to Reliabs

1

Connect Your Asana Workspace

In Reliabs Settings, go to Integrations then Asana. Authorize via Asana OAuth. Reliabs requests read access to projects and tasks in the teams you select.

2

Select Projects to Monitor

Choose which Asana projects Reliabs should monitor. You can select all projects or focus on the ones tied to active product bets.

3

Link Projects to Bets

From any product bet, add the relevant Asana project as a dependency tracker. Reliabs monitors milestones and flags delivery risk automatically.

4

Configure Risk Thresholds

Set your sensitivity for delivery risk alerts — e.g., flag when a milestone is more than 3 days late or when task volume exceeds baseline by 50%.

FAQs

Reliabs + Asana — Common Questions

Does Reliabs work with Asana personal plans?
+

Reliabs works with Asana Premium, Business, and Enterprise plans which include full API access. The Asana Basic plan has limited API access that may restrict some integration features.

Can Reliabs create tasks in Asana?
+

Reliabs is read-only for Asana by default. On select plans with write-back enabled, Reliabs can create new Asana tasks from product bet actions — but this is strictly opt-in.

Can I connect multiple Asana workspaces?
+

Reliabs supports connecting a single Asana workspace per account at launch. Multi-workspace support is planned for a future release.
