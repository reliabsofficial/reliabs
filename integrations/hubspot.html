Reliabs + HubSpot

Your HubSpot pipeline tells a story about what product gaps are slowing deals and what messaging resonates with buyers. Reliabs reads that story and surfaces it as ranked product opportunities — bridging product and GTM teams like never before.

What This Integration Does

The Reliabs + HubSpot integration reads opportunity data, deal stages, and contact activity from HubSpot. Product gaps logged by sales reps and marketing feedback from campaigns are scored by revenue impact and surfaced as ranked product opportunities in your roadmap.

Setup

How to Connect HubSpot to Reliabs

1

Connect Your HubSpot Account

In Reliabs Settings, go to Integrations then HubSpot. Click Connect and authorize via HubSpot OAuth. Reliabs requests read access to deals, contacts, and companies.

2

Map Product Feedback Fields

Tell Reliabs which HubSpot fields contain product feedback — loss reasons, feature request fields, or custom properties. Reliabs extracts signals from those fields automatically.

3

Set Revenue Thresholds

Define what deal size qualifies as a major signal. Reliabs will prioritize product gaps from high-value HubSpot opportunities automatically.

4

Review First Signal Batch

Reliabs surfaces your first set of HubSpot-originated product opportunities with revenue evidence attached. Review, link to bets, or dismiss false positives.

FAQs

Reliabs + HubSpot — Common Questions

Does Reliabs modify HubSpot data?
+

No. Reliabs is read-only for HubSpot. It reads deal data, contact notes, and custom fields — but never modifies records, changes deal stages, or creates contacts.

Which HubSpot plans does Reliabs support?
+

Reliabs works with HubSpot Professional and Enterprise plans, which include the API access needed for integration. Starter plans have limited API access.

Can I use HubSpot and Salesforce in Reliabs at the same time?
+

Yes. Teams running both CRMs can connect both. Reliabs correlates signals from both sources and gives you a unified revenue-signal view without duplication.
