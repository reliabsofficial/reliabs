Step-by-Step Guide

How to Migrate from Productboard to Reliabs

Most teams complete this migration in a single working session. Here is the full process.

1

Export Your Productboard Data

In Productboard, go to Settings → Export. Export your features, insights, and objectives in CSV format. Export each section separately: Features, Objectives, and Customer Insights.

2

Create Your Reliabs Workspace

Join the Reliabs waitlist and request early access with migration priority. Once access is granted, create your workspace and configure your product areas to match your Productboard hierarchy.

3

Import via Reliabs Migration Tool

In Reliabs Settings → Import, select "Migrate from Productboard." Upload your exported CSVs. Reliabs maps your Productboard features to product bets, preserving hierarchy and status.

4

Reconnect Your Integrations

Go to Reliabs Settings → Integrations and reconnect each tool: Jira, Intercom, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Salesforce. Your existing data in those tools remains intact — Reliabs re-indexes it for your new workspace.

5

Invite Your Team

Go to Settings → Team and invite your team members via email. Reliabs supports role-based permissions: PM, Viewer, and Admin roles map intuitively from Productboard's permission model.

6

Run Your First Reliabs Briefing

With integrations connected, trigger your first weekly briefing. Reliabs will synthesize signals from all your connected tools and deliver a ranked list of product opportunities — your first experience of the Reliabs intelligence layer.

FAQs

Migration FAQs

How long does the migration take?
+

Most teams complete the entire migration — data import, integration reconnection, and team invites — in a single working session of 2 to 4 hours. We provide a dedicated migration checklist and a Reliabs team member to assist during your first session.

Do I lose my Productboard data?
+

No. Productboard exports a full copy of your data. Your Productboard workspace remains untouched throughout the migration — you can run both tools in parallel before fully switching over.

Will my Jira integration be disrupted?
+

No. Your Jira data remains in Jira throughout the migration. You simply reconnect Jira to Reliabs — the integration is re-established with your existing Jira data intact. Productboard's Jira connection can be disconnected after you have verified the Reliabs connection is working correctly.

Can I run Productboard and Reliabs at the same time during transition?
+

Yes, and we recommend it. Run both in parallel for 2 to 4 weeks, using Reliabs for new work while your existing Productboard roadmap continues. Once your team is comfortable, complete the cutover.

Is there a migration support service?
+

Yes. Early access Reliabs users who are migrating from Productboard receive dedicated migration support. Join the waitlist and mention your migration when you sign up — we will prioritize your onboarding.
