Reliabs + Slack
Product Intelligence Where Your Team Lives.

Bring Reliabs into Slack and get your weekly briefings, capture signals from any channel, and query your roadmap — without ever opening a new tab. Product intelligence delivered where your team already spends its day.

What This Integration Does

The Reliabs Slack integration delivers your ranked weekly product briefing to a designated channel, lets you capture signals by reacting to messages with an emoji, and gives your team a Reliabs bot to query roadmap status on demand.

Setup

How to Connect Slack to Reliabs

1

Install the Reliabs Slack App

In Reliabs Settings, go to Integrations, then Slack. Click Add to Slack and authorize the app in your workspace. The Reliabs bot appears in your Apps sidebar.

2

Choose Delivery Channels

Select which Slack channels receive weekly briefings, signal alerts, and bot responses. You can create a dedicated #reliabs channel or use existing ones.

3

Set Your Capture Emoji

Choose the emoji that triggers signal capture. The default is a pushpin, but customize it to match your team's workflow.

4

Select Monitored Channels

Choose which channels Reliabs monitors passively for product signals. Reliabs surfaces conversation themes from those channels into your weekly signal feed.

FAQs

Reliabs + Slack — Common Questions

Does Reliabs read all my Slack messages?
+

No. Reliabs only reads messages in channels you explicitly select for monitoring, and only captures signals from messages you react to with the capture emoji. It does not passively read all workspace messages.

Can I restrict who can interact with the Reliabs Slack bot?
+

Yes. You can restrict Reliabs bot commands to specific users or channels. By default, anyone in the workspace can query the bot, but this is configurable per workspace.

Is the Reliabs Slack app compatible with Enterprise Grid?
+

Yes. Reliabs follows Slack security guidelines and is compatible with Slack Enterprise Grid. Enterprise Slack admins may need to approve the app installation before team members can use it.
