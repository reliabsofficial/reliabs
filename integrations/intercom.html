Customer Conversations as Product Intelligence.

Connect Intercom to Reliabs and transform thousands of customer conversations into ranked, evidence-backed product opportunities — automatically. Stop reading every thread. Start seeing the patterns your product needs to solve.

What This Integration Does

Setup

How to Connect Intercom to Reliabs

1

Connect Your Intercom Workspace

In Reliabs Settings, go to Integrations, then Intercom. Click Connect and authorize with your Intercom credentials. Reliabs requests read access to conversations, contacts, and tags.

2

Select Conversation Sources

Choose which inboxes, tags, or conversation types Reliabs should analyze. You can include all conversations or just product-feedback-tagged ones.

3

Set Clustering Sensitivity

Tell Reliabs how aggressively to cluster themes — conservative for strong signals only, or broad to catch emerging patterns early. Adjustable anytime.

4

Review Your First Batch

Reliabs processes your last 90 days of Intercom conversations and surfaces your first set of clustered product opportunities. Review, link to roadmap bets, or discard false positives.

FAQs

Reliabs + Intercom — Common Questions

Does Reliabs read all Intercom conversations?
+

Reliabs reads only the conversations in the inboxes and with the tags you select. You control scope — you can limit it to specific inboxes or only conversations tagged as product feedback.

Is customer data kept private?
+

Yes. Reliabs processes conversation data only to extract product signals. It does not store full transcripts — only anonymized theme clusters and quotes you have approved for roadmap context.

How often does Reliabs process new Intercom conversations?
+

Reliabs processes new conversations daily by default. With a high-frequency plan, near-real-time processing is available for teams with large conversation volumes.
