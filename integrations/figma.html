Design Integration

Reliabs + Figma
Design Is Part of Strategy, Not a Side Effect.

Design progress is invisible in most product tools. Reliabs fixes that — Figma files are linked directly to product bets, so you always know where design stands relative to delivery.

What This Integration Does

The Reliabs + Figma integration links Figma files, frames, and components to your roadmap bets. When a designer updates a file, Reliabs captures the change and updates the associated product bet status — closing the design-to-delivery visibility gap.

Setup

How to Connect Figma to Reliabs

1

Connect Your Figma Account

In Reliabs Settings → Integrations → Figma, click "Connect with Figma." Authenticate via OAuth. Reliabs requests read access to your team files.

2

Select Your Team & Projects

Choose which Figma teams and projects Reliabs should index. You can include all projects or just your active product design files.

3

Link Files to Roadmap Bets

From any product bet in Reliabs, click "Add Design" and search for the related Figma file. Reliabs creates the link and starts monitoring for changes.

4

Set Notification Preferences

Choose when you want design change alerts — on every update, or only on major version changes. Get notified in Reliabs or via Slack.

FAQs

Reliabs + Figma — Common Questions

Does Reliabs require a paid Figma plan?
+

Reliabs works with Figma Professional and Organization plans. The Figma Starter plan has limited API access that may restrict some integration features. Figma Business and Enterprise plans are fully supported.

Can Reliabs view private Figma files?
+

Only files explicitly shared with or accessible by the connected Figma account. Reliabs respects Figma's access controls — it cannot view files the authenticated user cannot view.

Does Reliabs modify Figma files?
+

No. Reliabs is read-only with respect to Figma. It reads file metadata and previews but never writes to, renames, or modifies your Figma files.
