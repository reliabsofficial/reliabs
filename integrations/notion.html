Reliabs + Notion

The best product context lives in Notion docs that nobody reads before big decisions. Reliabs changes that — indexing your Notion workspace and surfacing the right context at the right moment in your roadmap workflow.

What This Integration Does

The Reliabs + Notion integration indexes your Notion databases and pages, making them searchable from inside Reliabs and linkable directly to roadmap bets. When a strategy doc is updated, Reliabs detects the change and flags any relevant product bets for review.

Setup

How to Connect Notion to Reliabs

1

Connect Your Notion Workspace

In Reliabs Settings, go to Integrations then Notion. Click Connect. Authorize via OAuth. Reliabs requests access only to pages you explicitly select.

2

Select Pages to Index

Choose which Notion pages and databases Reliabs should index. Reliabs respects Notion's granular permission structure entirely.

3

Link Key Docs to Bets

From any product bet, click Add Context, then Notion, and search for the related strategy doc. Reliabs monitors linked docs for updates.

4

Set Up Database Monitoring

For structured Notion databases like a feature request tracker, configure Reliabs to read it as a recurring signal source.

FAQs

Reliabs + Notion — Common Questions

Does Reliabs index all my Notion pages?
+

Only the pages you explicitly share during OAuth setup. Reliabs respects Notion's granular page sharing — you control exactly what it can access.

Can Reliabs create or edit Notion pages?
+

Reliabs is read-only for Notion by default. An optional write-back feature can create pages from Reliabs product briefs, but this is strictly opt-in.

Does Reliabs work with Notion databases?
+

Yes. Reliabs reads both standard pages and structured databases. Database integration is particularly powerful for teams who maintain feature request trackers or OKR tables in Notion.
