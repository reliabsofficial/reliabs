Customer Support Integration

Reliabs + Zendesk

Your Zendesk ticket queue is a goldmine of product signals that most teams never systematically mine. Reliabs reads ticket volume, categories, and resolution times — surfacing the bugs and product gaps that create the most friction for your users.

What This Integration Does

The Reliabs + Zendesk integration reads your support ticket taxonomy, volume trends, and escalation patterns. Recurring issue categories are clustered into product opportunities with ticket count, resolution time, and affected account tier as supporting evidence.

Capabilities

What Reliabs + Zendesk Unlocks

01

Ticket Theme Clustering

Reliabs clusters Zendesk tickets by product area and issue type. See which problems appear most frequently — ranked by volume and customer impact.

02

Escalation Signal Detection

When ticket escalation rate spikes for a specific issue category, Reliabs flags it as a high-priority product signal with supporting ticket data.

03

Resolution Time as Signal

Tickets with consistently long resolution times often indicate a missing product feature. Reliabs surfaces these patterns as product opportunities.

04

Account-Weighted Impact

Enterprise support tickets are weighted higher. Reliabs knows account tier from Zendesk contact data — high-value customer friction rises to the top.

05

Cross-Tool Correlation

Zendesk ticket themes are correlated with Jira bugs and Mixpanel usage drops. Reliabs shows you when multiple tools point to the same product problem.

06

Ticket Volume Forecasting

See which product areas are trending toward higher ticket volume — letting you get ahead of support spikes with roadmap interventions.

Setup

How to Connect Zendesk to Reliabs

1

Connect Your Zendesk Account

In Reliabs Settings, go to Integrations then Zendesk. Click Connect and authorize. Reliabs requests read access to tickets, ticket fields, and user data.

2

Select Ticket Types to Monitor

Choose which ticket forms, tags, or categories Reliabs should analyze. Start with your highest-volume categories and expand the scope over time.

3

Configure Impact Weighting

Tell Reliabs how to weight ticket signals — by volume alone, or by a combination of volume, account tier, and resolution time.

4

Review Clustered Opportunities

Reliabs surfaces your first grouped opportunities from Zendesk ticket data. Review the clusters, add context, and link them to existing roadmap bets.

FAQs

Reliabs + Zendesk — Common Questions

Does Reliabs read all Zendesk tickets?
+

Reliabs reads the ticket categories, tags, and fields you select during setup. You control the scope — you can limit analysis to specific ticket forms or tags.

Is customer PII from Zendesk stored by Reliabs?
+

Reliabs processes ticket data to extract product signals — it does not store personally identifiable customer information. Only anonymized theme clusters and aggregate metrics are retained.

Can Reliabs create Zendesk tickets?
+

Reliabs is read-only for Zendesk. It reads and analyzes tickets but never creates, modifies, or closes tickets in your Zendesk account.
