FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using Reliabs, from connections to decision logic. Still unsure? Drop us a message and we’ll walk you through it.

How is Reliabs different from normal analytics or dashboards?
+

Dashboards show you charts and fragmented signals, usually in separate tools. Reliabs combines them, ranks opportunities with evidence, and explains why a bet deserves a spot on your roadmap.

How do Auto-Drafted Plans work?
+

Reliabs automatically drafts plans that sound like your product team. It learns your language, metrics, and context across tools, then writes problem statements, hypotheses, and success metrics based on real data. When a new signal appears, you’ll see a ready-to-edit plan you can approve, refine, or ignore. It’s the fastest way to stay rigorous without writing from scratch.

Can we still work in our existing tools after connecting to Reliabs?
+

Yes. Any opportunities, plans, or tickets created from Reliabs sync back into your tools like Jira, Linear, or Notion. You can keep working there, and Reliabs will track status, evidence, and impact behind the scenes. Your existing workflows stay intact.

How does Reliabs learn our goals and priorities?
+

When you connect your stack, Reliabs analyses usage, tickets, deals, and feedback to understand which segments, metrics, and features matter most. You can tune priorities whenever you want. Reliabs keeps learning from outcomes so its rankings get sharper over time.

Is our product data private and secure?
+

Yes. Your data remains encrypted in transit and at rest. Reliabs uses secure, enterprise-grade practices and never sells your information. AI only processes your context to power decisions, not to train any external models.

What happens when our team joins the waitlist?
+

You’ll get early access as soon as your batch is ready. We’re onboarding in waves to keep performance tight and gather feedback from serious teams, including Heads of Product, Staff PMs, and founders who live inside product decisions.
