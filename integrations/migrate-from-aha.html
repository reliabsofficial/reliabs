Migrating from Aha.io to Reliabs

Aha.io is robust for strategy documentation. Reliabs is built for intelligence — synthesizing live signals from your entire tool stack, auto-drafting product plans, and tracking which bets actually paid off. Here is how to make the move.

Your Aha.io roadmaps, features, goals, initiatives, ideas, and all existing integration connections can be migrated to Reliabs. The process is structured and guided — most teams complete it in one session without data loss.

Step-by-Step Guide

How to Migrate from Aha.io to Reliabs

1

Export Your Aha.io Data

In Aha.io, go to Account Settings → Export. Export your Features list, Goals, Initiatives, and Ideas in CSV format. Aha.io provides granular export controls — export each section separately for the cleanest import experience.

2

Create Your Reliabs Workspace

Join the Reliabs waitlist with migration priority flagged. Once access is granted, create your workspace and configure product areas to mirror your Aha.io product line structure.

3

Import via Reliabs Migration Tool

In Reliabs Settings → Import, select "Migrate from Aha.io." Upload your exported CSV files. Reliabs maps your Aha.io Features to product bets and Goals to roadmap objectives — preserving hierarchy and delivery status.

4

Reconnect Your Integrations

Reconnect each integration in Reliabs Settings → Integrations. Start with Jira and Salesforce as these carry the most structural dependencies. Reliabs re-indexes existing data from each connected tool for your new workspace.

5

Invite Your Team and Set Roles

Invite team members via email from Settings → Team. Reliabs roles (PM, Viewer, Admin) map intuitively from Aha.io's permission model. All team members keep their existing integrations access.

6

Run Your First Intelligence Session

With integrations live, trigger your first Reliabs weekly briefing. The AI synthesizes signals from all connected tools and delivers your first ranked list of product opportunities — the Reliabs intelligence layer working from day one.

FAQs

Migration FAQs

How long does migration from Aha.io take?
+

Most teams complete the full migration in 3 to 5 hours. Aha.io's export format is well-structured, which makes import mapping straightforward. A Reliabs team member is available to assist during your first migration session.

Will my Aha.io Ideas portal data be lost?
+

No. Aha.io Ideas can be exported and imported into Reliabs where they are re-ingested as product signals. Reliabs clusters similar ideas and surfaces the most frequent themes as ranked product opportunities with your original ideas preserved as evidence.

What happens to my Aha.io Jira integration?
+

Your Jira data remains intact in Jira. During migration, you reconnect Jira to Reliabs — your existing epics, issues, and links re-sync to the new workspace. The Aha.io Jira connection can be removed after you confirm the Reliabs connection is working correctly.

Can I run Aha.io and Reliabs in parallel during transition?
+

Yes. We recommend a 2 to 4 week parallel period. Use Reliabs for new bets and intelligence sessions while Aha.io handles existing workflow. Once your team adjusts, complete the cutover and cancel your Aha.io subscription.

Does Reliabs have Aha.io's Strategy Map equivalent?
+

Reliabs handles strategy through its OKR and objectives layer, which maps to Aha.io's Goals and Initiatives. The visual strategy map format is not a direct equivalent in Reliabs — Reliabs prioritizes intelligence and decision speed over documentation-heavy strategy visualization.
