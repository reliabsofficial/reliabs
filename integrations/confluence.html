Docs and Wiki Integration

Reliabs + Confluence

Your Confluence wiki holds decisions, specs, and institutional knowledge that rarely informs the roadmap at the moment it matters. Reliabs changes that — indexing your Confluence spaces and making them a searchable, linked layer of your product intelligence.

What This Integration Does

The Reliabs + Confluence integration indexes your Confluence spaces and pages. Product specs, decision records, and architecture docs are made searchable from inside Reliabs and linkable to active roadmap bets — giving your team the context it needs before decisions, not after.

Setup

How to Connect Confluence to Reliabs

1

Connect Your Confluence Site

In Reliabs Settings, go to Integrations then Confluence. Click Connect and authorize via Atlassian OAuth. Reliabs requests read access to the spaces you select.

2

Select Spaces to Index

Choose which Confluence spaces Reliabs should index. You can include all spaces or limit to product and engineering spaces specifically.

3

Link Specs to Roadmap Bets

From any product bet, add a Confluence page as context. Reliabs monitors linked pages for updates and notifies you of changes.

4

Configure Label-Based Signals

Tell Reliabs which Confluence page labels indicate a product-relevant document — e.g., 'product-spec', 'decision-record'. Reliabs indexes labeled pages as signal sources.

FAQs

Reliabs + Confluence — Common Questions

Does Reliabs index public or private Confluence pages?
+

Reliabs respects Confluence's permission model entirely. It only indexes pages accessible to the Atlassian account used for authorization — private pages remain private.

Can Reliabs work with both Confluence and Notion?
+

Yes. Many teams use both — Confluence for engineering wikis and Notion for product docs. Reliabs indexes both and surfaces the right context from whichever tool contains it.

Does Reliabs support Confluence Data Center?
+

At launch, Reliabs supports Confluence Cloud. Confluence Data Center support is planned for a future release.
