Reliabs + Salesforce
Build What Closes Deals.

Your sales team knows which product gaps are costing deals. Reliabs reads those signals from Salesforce and surfaces them in your roadmap — ranked by revenue at stake, not by whoever shouted loudest in the last all-hands.

What This Integration Does

The Reliabs + Salesforce integration reads opportunity data, deal blockers noted in CRM fields, and lost deal reasons from Salesforce. These signals are scored, tagged, and surfaced as product opportunities — telling you which roadmap bets have the highest revenue justification.

Setup

How to Connect Salesforce to Reliabs

1

Connect via Salesforce OAuth

In Reliabs Settings → Integrations → Salesforce, click "Connect." You'll be redirected to Salesforce for OAuth authorization. Reliabs requests read-only access to Opportunities, Accounts, and custom fields.

2

Map Deal Blocker Fields

Tell Reliabs which Salesforce fields contain product feedback (e.g., "Loss Reason," "Feature Requests," custom fields). Reliabs reads those fields and extracts product signals.

3

Set Revenue Thresholds

Define what deal size qualifies as a "major signal" — Reliabs will prioritize product gaps from high-value opportunities automatically.

4

Review and Link Opportunities

Reliabs surfaces its first batch of sales-originated product opportunities. Review them, link to existing roadmap bets, or create new ones — with revenue evidence already attached.

FAQs

Reliabs + Salesforce — Common Questions

Does Reliabs modify any Salesforce data?
+

No. Reliabs is strictly read-only with Salesforce. It reads opportunity data, account fields, and custom product feedback fields — but never modifies records, creates leads, or changes opportunity stages.

Which Salesforce editions does Reliabs support?
+

Reliabs supports Salesforce Professional, Enterprise, Performance, and Unlimited editions. The Essentials plan's API access limitations may restrict some features.

Can sales reps see my product roadmap through the Salesforce integration?
+

The integration is one-directional for data flow — Salesforce data goes to Reliabs, not the reverse. If you want to share roadmap status with sales, that's a separate, opt-in Reliabs feature with granular access controls.
