Roadmap Meets Engineering Reality.

Jira is where your engineers live. Reliabs is where your product strategy lives. With the native integration, the two finally talk to each other — automatically, bidirectionally, in real time.

What This Integration Does

The Reliabs + Jira integration pulls epics, issues, and sprint data into your product roadmap. When you update priorities in Reliabs, those changes push back to Jira as updated ticket labels, priorities, or status changes — no copy-pasting, no Slack DMs to update engineers.

Setup

How to Connect Jira to Reliabs

Takes under 5 minutes. No engineering help needed.

1

Connect Your Jira Workspace

Go to Reliabs Settings → Integrations → Jira. Click "Connect" and authorize with your Atlassian account. Reliabs requests read/write access to your projects.

2

Select Your Projects

Choose which Jira projects to sync. You can select specific boards or all active projects. Reliabs will immediately begin indexing your epics and issues.

3

Map Epics to Product Bets

Reliabs auto-suggests which Jira epics match your existing roadmap bets. Accept suggestions or manually map them — your call.

4

Set Your Sync Rules

Choose bidirectional (recommended) or one-way sync. Set which Reliabs priority levels map to which Jira priority labels. Then you're live.

FAQs

Reliabs + Jira — Frequently Asked Questions

Does Reliabs sync Jira epics or only individual issues?
+

Reliabs syncs both epics and individual issues. Epics are mapped to roadmap bets, while individual tickets contribute signal data (volume, velocity, labels) that informs your roadmap intelligence.

Is the Jira sync real-time or batched?
+

Reliabs syncs with Jira in near real-time using webhooks. Status changes, new issues, and priority updates propagate within minutes — not overnight batch jobs.

Will my Jira data be modified by Reliabs?
+

Only if you enable write-back. In read-only mode, Reliabs only reads your Jira data. With write-back enabled, Reliabs can update ticket priorities and labels when you push from the roadmap — you control this setting completely.

Does Reliabs work with Jira Cloud and Jira Server?
+

At launch, Reliabs supports Jira Cloud. Jira Data Center / Server support is on the roadmap for Q3 2025.

Can I use Reliabs without connecting Jira?
+

Yes. Jira is one of 15+ integrations, not a requirement. Reliabs works with whatever tools you connect — even a single analytics tool will generate product signals.
