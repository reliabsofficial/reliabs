The Modern PM Stack — What It Looks Like in 2025

A product manager in 2025 touches more tools than ever. The average PM we surveyed uses 7.3 tools regularly — and spends roughly 90 minutes per day switching between them to assemble context that should be available in one place.

The best PM stacks in 2025 share one characteristic: they are connected. Tools talk to each other. A Jira status update surfaces in your roadmap view. A Mixpanel retention drop links to the related feature work. A Salesforce deal blocker becomes a product opportunity ranked by ARR.

The Single Most Important Tool Decision You Will Make

Every PM we spoke with agrees on this: the most impactful single tool decision you make is not which analytics tool to use or which project tracker to pick. It is what sits at the center of your stack and connects everything else.

For most teams today, that role is played by a mess of Notion docs, Slack threads, and weekly syncs. The emerging answer — and what Reliabs is purpose-built to be — is a product intelligence layer that reads from all your tools and surfaces the signals that deserve attention, ranked by evidence.

When your roadmap tool reads live from Mixpanel, Jira, Salesforce, and Intercom — and synthesizes those signals into a ranked list of product opportunities with evidence attached — you get back the 90 minutes per day you are currently spending switching tabs. That is the stack architecture worth optimizing toward in 2025.

How to Build a PM Stack That Actually Talks to Each Other

Three principles make the difference between a connected PM stack and a collection of disconnected tabs:

1. Start with your engineering layer and work outward

Jira or Linear is where product decisions become code. Start there and connect your roadmap tool bidirectionally. Everything else — analytics, feedback, CRM — feeds into why you are prioritizing what engineering is building.

2. Pick one analytics source of truth, then add a second for correlation

Most teams run Mixpanel or Amplitude as their primary product analytics tool. The insight most miss: running both and correlating their signals reveals patterns invisible in either tool alone. Reliabs reads from both simultaneously and surfaces the correlation automatically.

3. Make your roadmap tool the reader, not just the writer

Most PM tools are write tools — you log decisions and track features. The next generation of PM tools reads from your existing tool stack and synthesizes what it finds. When your roadmap tool reads from Jira, Mixpanel, Salesforce, and Intercom — you spend your time deciding, not gathering context.
