Project Tracking Integration

Reliabs + Monday.com

Monday.com tracks how work gets done. Reliabs tracks why the work matters. Connect the two and your team always has the full picture — execution context alongside product outcomes, in one unified view with zero duplication.

What This Integration Does

The Reliabs + Monday.com integration reads your boards, items, and status updates and connects them to product roadmap bets in Reliabs. As work progresses in Monday, bet delivery status updates in Reliabs — giving PMs a single source of truth for both strategy and execution.

Capabilities

What Reliabs + Monday.com Unlocks

01

Board-to-Bet Sync

Link Monday.com boards to Reliabs product bets. Board status updates flow into your roadmap delivery view automatically.

02

Item Progress Tracking

Individual Monday items tied to a product bet are monitored. When a key item slips, Reliabs flags the delivery risk to the PM.

03

Status Column Inference

Reliabs reads Monday status columns and maps them to roadmap delivery stages — in progress, blocked, done — without requiring manual configuration.

04

Timeline View Alignment

Monday timeline data is surfaced alongside your Reliabs roadmap. See planned vs. actual delivery at a glance.

05

Cross-Team Visibility

Different teams using Monday boards for the same product area are unified in Reliabs. Fragmented execution becomes one coherent delivery view.

06

Blockers as Product Signals

Items marked Stuck or Blocked in Monday surface as delivery signals in Reliabs — giving PMs visibility into blockers before they escalate.

Setup

How to Connect Monday.com to Reliabs

1

Connect Your Monday.com Account

In Reliabs Settings, go to Integrations then Monday.com. Authorize via Monday OAuth. Reliabs requests read access to the workspaces and boards you select.

2

Select Boards to Monitor

Choose which Monday boards correspond to active product bets. You can add boards at any time as new bets are created.

3

Map Status Columns

Tell Reliabs how to interpret your Monday status columns — which statuses mean In Progress, Done, or Blocked. Reliabs maps these to roadmap stages automatically.

4

Link Boards to Bets

From any product bet in Reliabs, add the corresponding Monday board as the execution tracker. Reliabs now monitors delivery for that bet.

Use Cases

How Teams Use Reliabs + Monday.com

Weekly Status Without Status Meetings

The PM opens Reliabs Monday morning. All Monday board statuses are reflected in roadmap bet delivery states. No status meeting needed — the picture is already current.

Blocker Escalation

Three items on the Monday marketing board are marked Stuck for 4+ days. Reliabs surfaces them as delivery risk on the linked product bet. The PM resolves the blocker before the weekly sync.

Cross-Team Launch View

A product launch involves 4 Monday boards — engineering, design, content, and ops. Reliabs aggregates all four into one launch readiness view for the PM — without accessing 4 separate boards manually.

FAQs

Reliabs + Monday.com — Common Questions

Does Reliabs support all Monday.com board types?
+

Reliabs supports Main boards and Shareable boards. Private boards require the connected account to have explicit access. Sub-items are supported on Monday Business plans and above.

Can Reliabs create items in Monday.com?
+

Reliabs is read-only for Monday by default. Write-back functionality — allowing Reliabs to create or update Monday items from product bet actions — is available on select plans.

Does Reliabs support Monday workdocs?
+

Monday workdoc support is on the integration roadmap. At launch, Reliabs reads board items, status columns, and timelines from Monday.com.
