Web Analytics Integration

Reliabs + Google Analytics

Google Analytics holds critical signals about how users find, enter, and drop off from your product. Reliabs surfaces GA conversion rates and traffic trends as product opportunities — giving web behavior its rightful place in roadmap decisions.

What This Integration Does

The Reliabs + Google Analytics integration reads your GA4 properties and monitors the metrics you select. Significant changes in traffic, conversion rates, and user flow patterns surface as product signals in Reliabs — correlated with your active roadmap bets automatically.

Landing Page Performance

Setup

How to Connect Google Analytics to Reliabs

1

Connect Your GA4 Property

In Reliabs Settings, go to Integrations then Google Analytics. Authorize via Google OAuth. Reliabs requests read-only access to your GA4 properties.

2

Select Key Metrics

Choose which GA4 metrics and dimensions Reliabs should monitor — conversions, user flow, traffic by source, page performance.

3

Set Alert Thresholds

Define significant change thresholds for each metric. Reliabs surfaces alerts with product context when thresholds are crossed.

4

Link Goals to Roadmap Bets

Attach GA4 conversion goals to the product bets designed to improve them. Reliabs tracks those goals post-launch to measure bet outcomes.

FAQs

Reliabs + Google Analytics — Common Questions

Does Reliabs support Universal Analytics?
+

Reliabs supports GA4 only. Google deprecated Universal Analytics in 2024. If your team is still on Universal Analytics, upgrading to GA4 is required before connecting to Reliabs.

Does Reliabs modify my GA4 data?
+

No. Reliabs is strictly read-only for Google Analytics. It reads metric data but never modifies goals, events, or audiences in your GA4 property.

Can Reliabs work with Google Search Console data too?
+

Google Search Console integration is on the Reliabs roadmap. At launch, Reliabs supports GA4 for web data.
