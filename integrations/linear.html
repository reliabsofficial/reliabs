Reliabs + Linear
Engineering Cycles Meet Product Strategy.

Your engineering team moves fast in Linear. Reliabs makes sure your product strategy moves with them. Link Linear cycles to roadmap bets, sync priorities automatically, and track outcomes when features ship — without a single manual update.

What This Integration Does

The Reliabs + Linear integration reads your Linear projects, cycles, and issues and connects them to corresponding roadmap bets. As a cycle progresses, bet delivery status updates in real time. When a cycle completes, Reliabs checks the linked outcome metrics and marks the bet result automatically.

Setup

How to Connect Linear to Reliabs

1

Connect Your Linear Workspace

In Reliabs Settings, go to Integrations then Linear. Click Connect and authorize with your Linear account. Reliabs requests read and write access to your teams and projects.

2

Select Teams to Sync

Choose which Linear teams Reliabs should monitor. You can include all teams or just the ones directly building the product roadmap items.

3

Map Projects to Bets

Reliabs auto-suggests which Linear projects match your existing roadmap bets. Accept suggestions or map manually based on your team's structure.

4

Enable Priority Sync

Choose whether Reliabs should push priority changes back to Linear. Bidirectional sync keeps both tools aligned automatically.

FAQs

Reliabs + Linear — Common Questions

Is Linear better than Jira for the Reliabs integration?
+

Both integrations are equally deep. The choice between Linear and Jira depends on your engineering team's preference — Reliabs supports both and can run them simultaneously for teams in transition between the two.

Does Reliabs sync Linear labels and priority levels?
+

Yes. Reliabs maps Linear priority levels (Urgent, High, Medium, Low) to Reliabs roadmap priority tiers. Label-based filtering is also supported for teams who use custom labels in Linear.

Can Reliabs create Linear issues from product bets?
+

Yes, on write-back plans. Reliabs can create a new Linear issue from a product bet with pre-populated title, description, and priority — reducing PM-to-engineering handoff friction.
